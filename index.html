    Two imaging problems where RGB models don’t transfer directly

    Oct 2026 – present

    CSIR – Central Scientific Instruments Organisation (CSIO)

    Terahertz imaging for non-destructive evaluation

    AI Research Intern – Terahertz (THz) Imaging & Computer Vision

    Developing a decoupled multi-stage AI pipeline that finds subsurface defects in FDM 3D-printed polymers from single-pixel raster THz reflection scans at 350 GHz and 500 GHz, 46 × 46 px at 1 mm per pixel resolution.

    AUROC
    0.992
    Average precision
    1.00
    Localization error
    1.0 mm
    Defect CNR
    5.3 → 8.2
    • Physics-aware preprocessing and dual-frequency fusion: edge-artifact masking, cross-band registration and interior-median scale normalization
    • Leave-one-scan-out benchmark of blind-defect segmentation across U-Net, XGBoost and unsupervised patch-memory anomaly detectors, with 1.0 mm localization under a 3–4 mm beam diffraction limit
    • Adapting DeepLabV3+ to low-resolution THz grids with output stride 8, compact ASPP dilation rates (1, 2, 4), Focal + Dice loss and beam-deconvolution post-processing, plus dual-band ratio features for material and infill classification

    PyTorch, SciPy, XGBoost, DeepLabV3+
